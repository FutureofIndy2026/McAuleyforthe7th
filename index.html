Patrick McAuley is the Republican nominee for the U.S. House of Representatives in Indiana's 7th District.

Since 2015, Patrick has been engaged at every level of government — from grassroots leadership here in Indiana to experience on Capitol Hill under the leadership of a current U.S. senator. He has seen firsthand how policy decisions affect families and their loved ones.

In January 2025, Patrick's team launched a district-wide survey that has received over 400 responses from voters across the 7th District. The message is clear. Families care about affordability, public safety, housing stability, career pathways, and the long-term future of our communities.

If we are going to bring change, we need to have the financial resources necessary to provide for public safety, housing, and creating great jobs for the next generation.

Using the relationships he has built over the course of fifteen years, Patrick and his team will work with state, local, and federal leadership to bring financial resources into our communities.
