Don't Talk About It, Be About It

Since 2008, homeownership rates across the district have stagnated at 54%, while the cost of owning a home has increased by nearly $134,000.00. During that same period, our district has lost approximately 45,000 jobs in construction, manufacturing and production, installation, maintenance, and repair occupations. As fewer people are able to access jobs and homes, the wealth gap grows.

At the same time, youth gun violence has increased by more than 900%.

Our team will update federal policy to give companies and small businesses more incentive to hire skilled workers. We will update policies and introduce legislation that create stronger pathways to homeownership. And we will work with local, state, and federal partners to end violence.

We must create policies that give the next generation a real opportunity.

That is our plan. And we will get it done.
